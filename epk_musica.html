50 Years of Audio Excellence

Behind the cinematic soundscapes of The Research Band is Eduardo Ramírez de Cartagena, a senior composer and producer with over 50 years of professional trajectory in the Spanish and European audiovisual industry.

Forged in the legendary studios of SINTONIA (creating bespoke audio for high-end advertising) and EXA / Philips Group (producing tailored soundtracks for Film and National Television), Eduardo brings a level of reliability, structural precision, and sonic depth that only decades of industry experience can provide.

Whether you need an evocative synth-wave score, a nostalgic orchestral arrangement, or a dark atmospheric soundscape, The Research Band delivers museum-quality audio, entirely composed, performed, and mastered in-house.
