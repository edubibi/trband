🌏 The Concept: East Meets West in High Fidelity

Conceived by Spanish veteran composer Eduardo Ramírez de Cartagena (founding member of iconic pop-rock band CADILLAC), A Journey of a Thousand Miles Begins with a Single Step draws its spirit directly from Chapter 64 of Lao Tzu’s Tao Te Ching.

"千里之行，始于足下 — A journey of a thousand miles begins beneath the feet."

Rather than a casual blend, the album constructs an authentic acoustic bridge: pristine traditional pentatonic arpeggios on the Guzheng, expressive string bends, and meditative Asian wind textures dialoguing intimately with the melodic depth of European 70s art-rock and modern downtempo Worldbeat rhythms.
