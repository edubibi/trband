## 3. COMPREHENSIVE TRACK-BY-TRACK BREAKDOWN

### TRACK 01: AS WE FALL
- **Format:** Vocal Song (Lead Male Vocal in English - Eduardo)
- **Primary Genre:** Synth-Pop / Sophisti-Pop / Cinematic Indie Pop
- **Subgenres:** 80s Heartbeat Pop, Adult Contemporary, Road-Trip Indie Rock
- **Tempo:** 96–98 BPM (Steady mid-tempo driving groove)
- **Time Signature:** 4/4
- **Key:** Major with modal minor tensions (nostalgic build with luminous resolution)
- **Sonic Profile:** 
  - *Rhythm Section:* Gated 80s-inspired kick and tight snare, driving 16th-note shakers/hats, stereo transition crashes.
  - *Bass:* Dual-layer architecture: warm analog sub-bass on verse sustains + tight electric bass for driving sections.
  - *Keys & Synths:* Lush chorused analog pads, delicate crystalline bell/plink arpeggios answering the vocal phrasing.
  - *Guitars:* Ambient clean electric guitars enriched with vintage tape echo.
  - *Vocals:* Intimate, up-close dry vocal delivery on verses blossoming into stacked, wide-panned vocal harmonies in the chorus.
- **Mood Keywords:** Cinematic, Driving, Nocturnal, Melancholy, Cathartic, Nostalgic, Hopeful.
- **Sounds Like (RIYL):** The War on Drugs, Bleachers, The Blue Nile, Chromatics, Phil Collins (ballad era).
- **Ideal Scene Fit:** Neon-lit highway driving at 4 AM, emotional departures, pivotal decisions, dramatic chapter endings.

### TRACK 02: DUCK, FROG AND ME
- **Format:** 100% INSTRUMENTAL (Features subtle background vocal harmonies / non-invasive textures)
- **Primary Genre:** Sophisti-Pop / Smooth Soul / Yacht Rock
- **Subgenres:** Instrumental R&B, Jazz-Pop, Chill Lounge
- **Tempo:** 86–88 BPM (Unhurried, elastic mid-tempo groove)
- **Time Signature:** 4/4 with gentle swing accentuation and off-beat syncopation
- **Key:** Modal / Minor with 7th, 9th, and suspended dominant extensions
- **Sonic Profile:**
  - *Rhythm Section:* Soft brushed snare (fat, dampened snare), round pillowy kick, delicate off-beat tambourine, congas, and shakers.
  - *Bass:* Warm melodic fretless / flatwound precision bass acting as a counter-melodic voice with smooth glissandos.
  - *Keys:* Tube-driven vintage Fender Rhodes / Wurlitzer running through stereo chorus; lush brass pads supporting the chorus width.
  - *Guitars:* Clean thumb-picked rhythm guitars with percussive muting on stereo flanks.
  - *Vocals:* Purely instrumental focus with ethereal, non-lyrical background vocal harmonies that add depth without clashing with screen dialogue.
- **Mood Keywords:** Sophisticated, Midnight Lounge, Dialogue-Safe, Velvet, Sultry, Warm, Relaxed, Organic, Retro 70s/80s.
- **Sounds Like (RIYL):** Steely Dan, Bob James, Stuff, Sade (Instrumental cuts), Michael McDonald, Crusaders.
- **Ideal Scene Fit:** Cocktail parties, luxury dining, late-night hotel bars, conversations where dialogue must remain 100% clear, upscale crime noir.
