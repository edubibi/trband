Executive Profile & Creator

Eduardo Ramírez de Cartagena is a prominent veteran composer, arranger, and multi-instrumentalist from Madrid, Spain. He is a historic co-founder of the renowned group CADILLAC (representing Spain at Eurovision 1986, earning multiple Gold and Platinum records, and touring extensively across Europe and Latin America).

Currently leading The Research Band (TRBAND), Eduardo presents this masterwork under his own independent label imprint, Khem Aura Records. All compositions, recordings, and publishing rights are 100% owned, free of third-party encumbrances, and registered with the Spanish Copyright Society (SGAE Member Nº 29.751).

Artistic Architecture & Concept

«A tree as big as a man’s embrace grows from a tiny sprout. A terrace nine stories high rises from a basket of earth. A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.» — Lao Tzu (Tao Te Ching, Chapter 64)

This album bridges two profound musical traditions:

  • European 1970s Melodic Progressive Heritage: Drawing from the lyrical, noble tradition of 1970s British progressive and lyrical rock, characterized by pastoral woodwinds, melodic fretless and analog basses, and expansive harmonic vocal polyphonies presented through contemporary electronic rhythms.
  • Classical Eastern Acoustic Timbre: Authentic master performances on the 21-string Guzheng (featuring intricate left-hand pitch-bending and modal pentatonics), the bamboo Dizi flute, and the expressive Erhu.
  • Contemporary Electronic Foundation: Pristine mid-tempo syncopations, tight sub-bass grooves, and ambient spatial reverbs designed for state-of-the-art audiophile listening.
